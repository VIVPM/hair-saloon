NOT LOVING IT? LET US KNOW.

Every stylist here takes real pride in their work. We listen carefully, pay attention to the details, and want you to walk out feeling great about your hair. But hair is personal, and once in a while something just isn't quite right.

Just tell us.

We'll happily get you back in the chair, talk it through and fix it. No awkwardness, no hurt feelings. An honest conversation is always better than living with hair you don't love.

Great hair comes from getting to know each other. Over time we learn your hair, you learn how to describe that one little thing you like, and your stylist becomes your stylist.

We're not after one great appointment. We're after a lot of them.
